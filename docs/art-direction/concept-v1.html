Penalty Night: character art direction, concept v1

Stylized but natural football players: real adult proportions (about 7.5 heads tall), bold readable silhouettes under floodlights, and kits with real detail (trim, numbers, glove straps, sock bands). Shown here: the in-game camera at the moment of a save, the result card, the character lineup, and one key pose for each animation the GLB models need.

Character lineup (same scale, head-unit guides)

Style rules

  • Proportions: 7.5 heads, broad shoulders, long legs. No chibi or big-head styling; the keeper is a head taller than the taker.
  • Silhouette first: limbs read clearly against the crowd at 140 px tall (keeper size in game). Oversized white gloves on the keeper so you can always find the hands.
  • Kits: flat team colour with one contrast trim (collar, cuffs, side panel), a number on the back, a band at the top of the socks. Same mesh for every team; only the textures change.
  • Surfaces: soft PBR (rough cloth, slightly glossy boots and gloves). Rim light from the floodlights separates players from the stands.
  • Faces: simple and readable, not photo-real. Nobody will see them up close except in replays.
